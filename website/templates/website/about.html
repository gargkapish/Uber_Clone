About RideNow

RideNow is a simple ride-booking website built with Django as a class project. It is inspired by ride-hailing apps but is not connected to any real company.

Our goal is to make getting around easy: pick a ride type, see the price, and book in a few clicks.

Why ride with us

  • Rides for every budget, from bikes to SUVs
  • Clear per-km pricing with no hidden charges
  • Verified drivers and safe trips
